# App Store Review Notes — PRISM

**Bundle ID:** `com.cortexnode.prism`  
**Version:** 1.0.0 (2026071201)  
**Category:** Social Networking / Productivity  

## What this app does

PRISM is a multi-channel draft studio with an approval gate and optional publish:

- Write a source signal on Home
- Refract to channel-specific drafts
- Draft queue + Approval Gate (Modules)
- Channels tab: connect Bluesky (app password) or gateway OAuth accounts
- Publish Queue: approve, then **Broadcast only to connected accounts**
- Unconnected platforms show **Not connected** — never auto-post
- Image Studio with local preset gallery and share sheet

## Demo flow for reviewer

1. Home → write signal → **Queue Refraction Preview**
2. **Modules → Draft Queue / Approval Gate** — Approve (syncs to Publish Queue)
3. **Channels** — optionally connect Bluesky with an app password, or leave disconnected
4. **Channels → Open Publish Queue** — if no accounts connected, Broadcast stays disabled with **Not connected**
5. **Studio** → Generate Preview → Share
6. **About** — publishing policy

## What is NOT automatic

- No auto-posting
- No broadcast without operator Approve + Broadcast
- No claim of live post when accounts are disconnected
- Blotato middleman — **retired**; PRISM is the publisher (Channels + Publish Queue)

## Approval gate (critical)

Nothing posts without explicit Approve. Broadcast is available only for platforms with a live connection in Channels.
